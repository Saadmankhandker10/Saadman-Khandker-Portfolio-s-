01 ABOUT

SPECIALIST IN the making.

Bilingual typing is my primary craft. I retype and rebuild documents in Bangla (Avro Unicode & Bijoy) and English — scanned PDFs, phone photos, handwritten notes, theses and reports — and deliver clean, styled MS Word files, at 65+ WPM and 99.9% accuracy across 50+ delivered commissions.

I'm Saadman Khandker (Khandker Sadman Rafid Saad), studying Graphic Design Technology at Govt. Graphic Arts Institute, Dhaka — which is why layout, typography and print-ready formatting matter to me as much as speed.

As Product Design & Web Intern at Classvel I build presentation decks and web content. Earlier, retail floor work at Agora Superstores and Meena Bazar taught me fast, accurate data entry under pressure.

Outside work I serve as a youth volunteer with the Bangladesh Red Crescent Society (BDRCS) — relief activities, field documentation and community events.
